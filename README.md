**Beyond Nuclear Tech II** is an industrial and space exploration modpack for Minecraft 1.12.2, combining heavy nuclear physics (**HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod CE**) and classic tech mods with deep space progression (**Galacticraft**).

### 🔗 Official Links
* **Download on CurseForge:** [Beyond Nuclear Tech II](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/beyond-nuclear-tech-ii)
* **Join our Discord Community:** [Official Discord Server](https://discord.gg/KDCPsMMc7)

### 🐛 Bug Reports & Suggestions
Found a recipe conflict, worldgen issue, or crash? Please open a new issue in the **[Issues](https://github.com/Kaiser-Vaticanov/Beyond-Nuclear-Tech-II/issues)** tab and attach your `crash-report` or `latest.log`.

### 📂 Repository Structure
* `overrides/config/` — Standardized ore generation, mod tweaks, and Custom Main Menu layouts.
* `overrides/resources/` — Custom Main Menu textures, logo, and slide-show assets.
* `manifest.json` & `modlist.html` — Full modlist and CurseForge manifest for the current release.
